## Sample bullets (fictional)

**Field sales (FMCG or distribution)**
- Achieved 118% of annual volume target across a 300-outlet territory in Lucknow
- Added 85 new retail outlets in 12 months, including 20 in previously unserved villages
- Cut distributor payment delays from 21 to 9 days by setting weekly collection visits

**Inside sales (SaaS)**
- Closed ₹1.8 crore in new annual contracts, 124% of quota, ranked 2nd of 18 reps
- Made 60+ calls a day; converted 14% of demos to paid plans
- Built an email sequence that lifted demo bookings by 30%

**Key account management**
- Managed 12 modern trade accounts worth ₹40 crore a year, growing them 16%
- Negotiated shelf placement in 140 stores, lifting category share by 2 points

**Business development**
- Signed 9 channel partners in 2 new states, contributing ₹6 crore in first-year sales

## Put the best number first

Recruiters often look at the first bullet of each role only. Lead with percentage of target or revenue.

## Skills

Prospecting, cold calling, negotiation, closing, pipeline management, CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho CRM), territory planning, distributor management, key account management, forecasting.

## No sales numbers yet?

Use what you can count: calls made, meetings booked, customers served, retention, cross-sell. See [quantifying achievements](/articles/how-to-quantify-achievements-on-resume/). For Indian field sales roles, see [field sales executive resume](/articles/field-sales-executive-resume/).
