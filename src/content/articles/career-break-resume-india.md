> Applying for jobs outside India? See [How to Explain an Employment Gap](/articles/employment-gap-on-resume/).


Career breaks are common in India, especially for women after marriage, relocation or childbirth, and for anyone caring for family. Many employers now run programmes specifically for people returning after a break.

## How to show the break

In your experience section, in date order:

```
Career break | Jun 2023 – Aug 2025
Family responsibilities. Completed GST practitioner course (2025) and helped manage
accounts for a relative's trading business.
```

You don't owe details. "Family responsibilities", "maternity and childcare", "relocation" or "health reasons, now resolved" are enough.

## Lead with your strengths

> Accountant with 6 years in accounts payable and GST compliance before a 2-year career break. Recently completed a GST practitioner course and refreshed Tally Prime skills. Looking to return to an accounts executive role.

## Refresh your skills

- Take one recognised course in your field
- Update tools: software versions and platforms change
- Do a small real project: freelance, volunteering, helping a family business

## Returnship and second-career programmes

Several large Indian and multinational employers run programmes for professionals returning after a break, often with mentoring and flexible starts. Search company careers pages for "returnship", "second career" or "career restart".

## In interviews

Say it in two sentences and move to why you're ready now. Practise it aloud.
