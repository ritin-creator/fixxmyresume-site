Recruiters search their ATS like a search engine: "Data Analyst" AND "SQL" AND "Power BI", maybe filtered by city. If those words aren't in your resume, you don't appear, however good you are.

## Step 1: Pull the keywords out

Paste the job description into a document and highlight:

- **The job title** (exact wording: "Business Analyst" vs "Business Systems Analyst")
- **Hard skills and tools** (Excel, SAP, Python, Tally, Salesforce)
- **Certifications and qualifications** (CA, PMP, AWS Certified, B.Tech)
- **Methods and domains** (Agile, GST compliance, B2B sales, inventory planning)
- **Phrases repeated two or more times**: they matter to the employer

Ignore filler words like "dynamic", "fast-paced" or "team player".

## Step 2: Check what's true for you

Make two lists: keywords you have and can prove, and ones you don't. Only the first list goes in. Interviewers will ask about anything listed.

## Step 3: Place them where they count

1. **Summary:** the job title and two or three top skills.
2. **Skills section:** the tools and methods, in the employer's spelling.
3. **Experience bullets:** the same skills in action. This is the most persuasive spot.

> Built weekly **Power BI** dashboards from **SQL** queries for 40 store managers, cutting manual reporting by 60%.

## Step 4: Match the wording

- If they say "Microsoft Excel", don't only write "spreadsheets".
- If they say "client relationship management", consider using that instead of "customer handling".
- Spell out acronyms once: "Search engine optimisation (SEO)".
- Use both forms for job titles if they differ: "Sales Executive (Field Sales)".

## Step 5: Don't overdo it

- No white-text keyword lists. Recruiters see them when the text is pasted, and some systems flag them.
- No repeating the same keyword ten times.
- No skills you can't discuss.

## A worked example

**Job description extract:** "We're hiring an Accounts Executive with 2+ years' experience in accounts payable, GST returns and bank reconciliation. Tally Prime and advanced Excel required."

**Keywords:** Accounts Executive, accounts payable, GST returns, bank reconciliation, Tally Prime, advanced Excel.

**Resume lines:**

- Summary: "Accounts executive with 3 years in accounts payable and GST returns for a mid-size distributor."
- Skills: "Tally Prime, Advanced Excel (pivot tables, XLOOKUP), GST returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B), bank reconciliation"
- Bullet: "Processed 400+ vendor invoices a month in Tally Prime and completed monthly bank reconciliation for 4 accounts."
