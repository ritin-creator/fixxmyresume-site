Most of India's job seekers aren't from the metros. Recruiters in large cities increasingly hire from smaller towns, especially for sales, operations, support and remote roles. A few resume choices make a big difference.

## 1. Lead with what you did, not where you studied

A recruiter may not know your college, but they understand "Added 64 new retail outlets in a year" or "Built an inventory app used by a 1,500-product pharmacy". Put your strongest numbers at the top.

## 2. Make relocation easy to see

Add it to the contact line:

> Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh | Open to relocate to Delhi NCR, Lucknow, Jaipur

Recruiters filter by location, so also set preferred locations on job portals.

## 3. Regional languages are a skill

For sales, support, collections and field roles across India, speaking Hindi, Bhojpuri, Marathi, Telugu, Bengali or Tamil matters. List them with fluency.

## 4. Fill gaps with free and low-cost proof

- NPTEL and SWAYAM courses
- Google, Microsoft or AWS certificates
- A small real project: a website for a local shop, accounts for a family business, a social media page for a coaching centre

## 5. Don't let a template do the talking

Common traps from cyber cafés and old templates:

- Father's name, religion, caste, date of birth and a "Declaration" with signature
- A big photo
- Long objectives about "a reputed organisation"
- Hobbies like "reading newspapers"

These belong to the old biodata style. See [biodata vs resume](/articles/biodata-vs-resume-vs-cv/).

## 6. Write in simple, correct English

Short sentences are fine. Recruiters don't expect fancy words; they expect clarity. Check spellings of your email, phone and company names twice.

## 7. Look at remote and hybrid roles

Customer support, content, data entry, inside sales and many tech roles can be done from anywhere. See [remote job resume](/articles/remote-job-resume/).
