LinkedIn is where many Indian recruiters search for experienced candidates, and increasingly for freshers too. A complete profile appears in more searches.

## 1. Photo and banner

Recent, clear, professional photo; plain background. Banner can be simple: your field, city or a work image.

## 2. Headline

Not just "Student at XYZ College". Use role + skills:

> Aspiring Data Analyst | SQL, Excel, Power BI | B.Com 2026, Delhi University

> Accounts Executive | Tally Prime, GST, TDS | 3 years in distribution

## 3. About section

Three to five short paragraphs:

1. What you do or want to do
2. Two or three results
3. Skills and tools
4. What you're looking for, and how to reach you

First person is fine here.

## 4. Experience

Same titles, companies and dates as your resume. Add two to four bullets with numbers. Add media: project links, certificates, presentations.

## 5. Skills and certifications

Add 10+ relevant skills; pin your top three. Add courses and certificates (NPTEL, Google, AWS, Coursera).

## 6. Open to Work

Turn it on with target job titles and cities. You can choose to show it only to recruiters.

## 7. Network

- Connect with alumni at target companies
- Follow companies you want to join
- Send a short note with connection requests: "Hi Neha, I'm a 2026 B.Tech graduate interested in backend roles at Northwind. Would value connecting."

## 8. Post occasionally

A project you finished, a certification, something you learned. Recruiters notice active profiles.

## 9. Match your resume

Recruiters compare both. See [LinkedIn profile vs resume](/articles/linkedin-profile-vs-resume/). You can also download your profile as a PDF (Profile > More > Save to PDF) and upload it to FixxMyResume to turn it into a resume.
