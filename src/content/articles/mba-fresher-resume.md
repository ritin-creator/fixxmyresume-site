## Structure

1. Name, contact, LinkedIn
2. Summary (role + specialisation + summer internship highlight)
3. Education (MBA/PGDM, then UG, then XII and X)
4. Summer internship (most space)
5. Work experience (if any, pre-MBA)
6. Live projects and case competitions
7. Positions of responsibility
8. Certifications and skills

## Summer internship bullets (fictional)

**Marketing (FMCG):**
- Mapped 120 retail outlets in 3 Indore markets and identified 18 underserved for a new snack SKU
- Recommended a scheme for small retailers adopted in 2 districts; pilot sales rose 14%

**Finance (BFSI):**
- Built a financial model for a ₹40 crore NBFC loan book; analysed portfolio risk by region
- Prepared credit appraisal notes for 12 MSME loan files

**HR:**
- Redesigned the onboarding checklist for 200 annual hires; cut joining-day issues by about half
- Analysed exit interviews from 2 years, identifying 3 top attrition reasons

**Operations:**
- Mapped picking process in a 40,000 sq ft warehouse; layout change raised picks per hour by 18%

## Case competitions and live projects

> National Finalist, Marketing Case Competition (fictional), 2025 | top 8 of 600 teams

## Positions of responsibility

> Placement Committee Coordinator | managed outreach to 70 companies for the final placement season

## Tips

- Use your college's format if it has one
- One page
- Pre-MBA work experience is a big plus: quantify it
- Avoid buzzwords; recruiters see hundreds of MBA resumes in a season
