- q: What should a project manager resume include?
  a: Project scope, budget, team size, timeline and results, plus methodologies (Agile, Scrum, Waterfall), tools such as Jira or MS Project, and certifications like PMP or PRINCE2.
- q: Should I list every project I managed?
  a: No. Pick the largest or most relevant three to five and quantify them. Group smaller ones in one bullet if needed.
- q: Do I need a PMP to get a project manager job?
  a: Not always, but it helps for many corporate roles. If you're studying for it, you can list 'PMP (in progress, exam scheduled for March 2027)'.

## Sample bullets (fictional)

**IT projects**
- Delivered an ERP rollout across 14 branches on a ₹4 crore budget, two weeks early and 6% under budget
- Ran Scrum for 3 teams (22 people), raising sprint delivery from 70% to 92% of committed points
- Managed vendor contracts worth ₹2.5 crore, renegotiating support terms to save ₹30 lakh a year

**Construction and infrastructure**
- Managed a ₹120 crore residential project (6 towers) from foundation to handover in 30 months
- Cut rework by 25% by introducing weekly quality walk-throughs with subcontractors

**Operations and process**
- Led a warehouse automation project that lifted picking rate from 80 to 130 orders an hour
- Coordinated 5 departments to move 600 employees to a new office over one weekend with no downtime

## What to show for each project

1. Scope (what was delivered)
2. Scale (budget, team size, users or sites)
3. Timeline and whether it was met
4. Outcome (savings, revenue, efficiency, quality)
5. Your role in risk, stakeholders and vendors

## Certifications to list if you have them

PMP, PRINCE2, Certified ScrumMaster (CSM), PMI-ACP, SAFe, ITIL, Lean Six Sigma.

## Skills

Planning, scheduling, budgeting, risk management, stakeholder management, vendor management, Agile, Scrum, Waterfall, Jira, MS Project, Smartsheet, reporting.
