Articleship is the practical training every CA student completes under a practising chartered accountant. Firms receive many applications, so a clear one-page resume helps. Check ICAI's current rules for when you're eligible to start and for how long, as they've changed over the years.

## What firms look for

1. Exam progress and marks (CA Foundation, Intermediate; exemptions; ranks)
2. Basic practical skills (Tally, Excel, GST)
3. Communication and willingness to travel for audits
4. Availability: start date and city

## Tips

- Mention cities you're willing to travel to for audits
- Big firms and mid-size firms differ: say which areas interest you
- Keep it honest; firms often test Tally and Excel in interviews
