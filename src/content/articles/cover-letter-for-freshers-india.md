## Format

```
Dear [Name / Hiring Manager],

[Role] at [Company]: why you're applying (one specific reason).
[Your strongest proof: project, internship or achievement, with a number.]
[Skills that match the job.]
[Close: request a conversation, thank them.]

[Name] | [Phone] | [Email]
```

## 1. Software developer (B.Tech)

> Dear Hiring Manager,
>
> I'm applying for the Associate Software Engineer role at Northwind Tech. I've been using your payments API in a college project and would like to help build it.
>
> In my final year, I built a placement portal API in Spring Boot used by 900 students and 40 recruiters, and during my internship I raised unit test coverage on a billing module from 45% to 70%.
>
> I'm comfortable with Java, SQL and Git, and keen to learn your stack. I'd welcome the chance to talk.

## 2. Accounts executive (B.Com)

> Dear Mr Gupta,
>
> I'm applying for the Accounts Executive (Fresher) role at Sharma Traders. During my 6-month internship at a CA firm, I entered 300+ invoices in Tally Prime, prepared GSTR-1 data for 4 clients and reconciled two bank accounts monthly. I'd be glad to bring the same care to your accounts team.

## 3. Sales trainee

> Dear Sir/Madam,
>
> I'm applying for the Sales Trainee role in Lucknow. As sponsorship head for our college fest, I raised ₹6 lakh from 14 local businesses, up from ₹3.5 lakh the year before. I speak Hindi and English, own a two-wheeler and am happy to travel across the territory.

## 4. Digital marketing intern or executive

> Dear Ms Kapoor,
>
> I'm applying for the Digital Marketing Executive role. I ran Instagram for our college fest, reaching 25,000 accounts in four weeks, and completed Google's digital marketing certificate. I'd like to bring that energy to your D2C brand.

## 5. HR executive (MBA HR)

> Dear Hiring Team,
>
> I'm applying for the HR Executive role. In my summer internship, I redesigned an onboarding checklist for 200 annual hires and analysed two years of exit interviews to find the top three reasons for attrition. I'd value the chance to discuss how I could support your hiring team.

## Short email or Naukri message

> Hi Neha, I'm a 2026 B.Com graduate with a 6-month accounts internship (Tally Prime, GST). I'd like to apply for the Accounts Executive role in Noida. Resume attached. Thank you, Amit, +91 94300 00000
