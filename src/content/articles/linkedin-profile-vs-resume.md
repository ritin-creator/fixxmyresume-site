## The differences

| | Resume | LinkedIn profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audience | One employer, one job | Recruiters searching, your network |
| Length | 1–2 pages | As long as useful |
| Voice | No "I", third-person feel | First person is normal in About |
| Tailoring | Changes per job | Stays general for your target field |
| Extras | Rarely | Recommendations, posts, media, certifications |

## What must match exactly

- Job titles
- Employer names
- Start and end dates
- Degrees and institutions

Recruiters check both. A mismatch looks careless at best and dishonest at worst.

## What can differ

- **Headline:** LinkedIn's headline is searchable. Use your target title and key skills: "Data Analyst | SQL, Power BI | Retail Analytics".
- **About:** a short first-person story with keywords.
- **Experience:** can include more bullets and context than your resume.
