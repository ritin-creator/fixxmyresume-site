faq:
- q: What is the most common resume mistake?
  a: Listing duties instead of results. 'Responsible for sales' says little; 'Grew monthly sales from ₹8 lakh to ₹11 lakh in 6 months' shows what you achieved.
- q: Do spelling mistakes really matter on a resume?
  a: Yes. Recruiters read errors as a lack of care, and ATS keyword searches can miss misspelt skills. Proofread, then ask someone else to read it.
- q: Is it a mistake to use a fancy resume template?
  a: Often. Two-column layouts, tables, icons and text boxes can scramble when an ATS reads the file. A clean single-column layout is safer.

Grouped by type, each with the fix.

## Layout and file

1. **Two-column templates.** ATS often merge the columns line by line. *Fix:* one column.
2. **Tables and text boxes.** Content can be skipped. *Fix:* plain lines and bullets.
3. **Contact details in the header or footer.** Some parsers ignore those areas. *Fix:* put them in the body.
4. **Skill bars and icons.** Unreadable to software, meaningless to people. *Fix:* plain skill names.
5. **Scanned or image PDFs.** No text to read. *Fix:* export from Word or Docs as PDF.
6. **File named "resume_final_v3.pdf".** *Fix:* `Firstname-Lastname-Resume.pdf`.

## Writing

7. **Duties instead of achievements.** "Responsible for…" *Fix:* verb + what + number + result.
8. **No numbers.** *Fix:* [quantify your achievements](/articles/how-to-quantify-achievements-on-resume/).
9. **A generic summary.** "Hardworking, passionate professional…" *Fix:* role + years + skills + result.
10. **First person.** "I managed…" *Fix:* start with the verb.
11. **Long paragraphs.** Recruiters skim. *Fix:* bullets of one to two lines.
12. **Mixed tenses.** *Fix:* past tense for past jobs, present for current.
13. **Buzzwords.** "Synergy", "go-getter", "thinking outside the box". *Fix:* show it with a result.

## Keywords and targeting

14. **One resume for every job.** *Fix:* [tailor it in 15 minutes](/articles/tailor-resume-to-job-description/).
15. **Missing the job title.** Recruiters search by title. *Fix:* put the target title in your summary.
16. **Skills you can't back up.** *Fix:* only list what you'd happily be tested on.
17. **Unexplained acronyms.** *Fix:* spell out once.

## Details

18. **Typos in your email or phone.** The most expensive typo there is. *Fix:* check twice, call your own number.
19. **Unprofessional email.** *Fix:* firstname.lastname@ on a common provider.
20. **Inconsistent dates.** "2019", "05/2021", "June 2022". *Fix:* one format, like "Jun 2022".
21. **Unexplained gaps.** *Fix:* one neutral line ([guide](/articles/employment-gap-on-resume/)).
22. **Too long.** *Fix:* one page under ~8 years ([length guide](/articles/how-long-should-a-resume-be/)).
23. **Personal details that invite bias.** Religion, caste, marital status, photo where not expected. *Fix:* leave them out unless required.
24. **"References available on request".** Everyone knows. *Fix:* delete it.
25. **Lying.** Fake degrees, titles or numbers come out in background checks. *Fix:* only true facts, presented well.
