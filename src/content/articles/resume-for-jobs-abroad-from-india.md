## Country comparison

| Country | Length | Photo | DOB / marital status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| United States | 1 page (2 if senior) | No | No | Called a resume; very achievement-focused |
| Canada | 1–2 pages | No | No | Mention work permit or PR status |
| United Kingdom | 2 pages | No | No | Called a CV; mention right to work |
| Ireland | 2 pages | No | No | Similar to the UK |
| Germany | 1–2 pages | Common, optional | Sometimes | Tabular "Lebenslauf" style is common |
| Netherlands, Nordics | 1–2 pages | Optional | No | Direct, concise |
| UAE, Saudi, Qatar | 2 pages | Common | Often | Nationality, visa status and notice period expected ([Gulf guide](/articles/gulf-job-resume-uae-saudi/)) |
| Australia, New Zealand | 2–3 pages | No | No | Referees often listed |
| Singapore | 1–2 pages | Optional | Sometimes | Mention pass eligibility or status if relevant |

## What to change from an Indian resume

1. **Remove biodata habits:** father's name, religion, declaration and signature.
2. **Explain Indian terms:** "CTC" and "lakh" mean little abroad. Write salaries only if asked, in local currency or USD. Use "₹1.2 crore (about US$145,000)" only if the scale matters.
3. **Grades:** convert to the local idea of a grade only if asked; otherwise give your CGPA with its scale ("8.2/10").
4. **Company context:** add a few words for Indian companies that aren't known abroad: "Northwind Foods (FMCG, ₹800 crore revenue)".
5. **Work authorisation:** state it plainly: "Requires visa sponsorship" or "Holds Canadian PR".
6. **Phone number:** include the country code.

## Spelling

US English (organize, analyze) for the US and Canada; UK English (organise, analyse) for the UK, Ireland, Australia, the Gulf and much of Asia.
