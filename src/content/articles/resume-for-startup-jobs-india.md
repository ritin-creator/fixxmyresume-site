## What founders and early hiring managers want

1. **Ownership:** you took something from idea to done
2. **Speed:** you ship and learn quickly
3. **Range:** you can step outside your job description
4. **Proof:** links to real work

## How to show it

**Outcome-first bullets:**
- "Launched WhatsApp ordering for a 3-store bakery chain in 2 weeks; it now brings 15% of orders"
- "Set up the company's first analytics dashboard in Metabase used by the whole team"
- "Took over vendor onboarding when the ops lead left; onboarded 40 vendors in a month"

**Range:** a short "Also did" line in a role: "Also ran hiring for 3 roles and wrote the help centre."

**Links:** GitHub, portfolio, product links, Substack or LinkedIn posts, app store pages.

**Side projects and freelancing:** a section of its own if they show initiative.

## What to cut

- Corporate filler: "liaised with cross-functional stakeholders to drive synergies"
- Long responsibility lists
- Certificates unrelated to the role

## Format

One page, single column, ATS-friendly (many startups use tools like Greenhouse, Lever or Zoho Recruit too). See the [ATS checklist](/articles/how-to-make-resume-ats-friendly/).

## Applying

Founders often read direct messages. A short LinkedIn note or email with one specific idea for their product can beat a portal application. See [cover letter examples](/articles/cover-letter-examples/) for a cold email format.
