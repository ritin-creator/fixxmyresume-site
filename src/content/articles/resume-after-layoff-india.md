Layoffs happen to strong performers, especially in tech, startups and during restructurings. Recruiters know this.

## On the resume

- List the job normally, with the end month.
- Keep your best achievements from that role.
- No need for the word "laid off". If there's a gap of a few months, that's normal.

Optional, if it helps context:

> Role made redundant in a company-wide restructuring (Mar 2026).

## Make the most of your advantage

You can join immediately. Mark "Immediate joiner" on job portals and in your resume's contact line.

## Explaining it

Two sentences, no blame:

> "The company cut 20% of roles across product and engineering in March. My team was one of them. I'm looking for a backend role where I can keep working on payments systems."

## A 30-day plan

| Week | Do |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Update resume, Naukri and LinkedIn; set "Open to work"; collect references and relieving documents |
| 2 | List 30 target companies; ask former colleagues for referrals |
| 3 | Apply to 5–10 well-matched roles a week with tailored resumes |
| 4 | Practise interviews; take a short course if a skill gap keeps coming up |

## Look after the basics

Check your full and final settlement, PF transfer, gratuity eligibility and health insurance continuity with HR.
