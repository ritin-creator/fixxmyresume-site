## Personal details Gulf employers expect

- Full name, phone with country code, email
- **Nationality:** Indian
- **Current location** and willingness to relocate
- **Visa status:** for example "In India, requires employment visa" or "UAE visit visa until 30 Dec 2026"
- **Availability / notice period**
- **Passport:** valid until (month and year)
- **Driving licence:** Indian, or GCC licence if you have one; this matters for sales and field jobs
- **Languages:** English, Hindi, Malayalam, Arabic (basic), etc.
- Photo

Date of birth and marital status are still commonly included in Gulf CVs, but only add them if the advert or agency asks.

## Experience

- Use internationally understood job titles ("Accountant", "Site Engineer", "Sales Executive")
- Name project scale and clients: "₹300 crore residential project" or "AED 20 million fit-out"
- Highlight any **GCC experience** first
- Mention international standards and tools: IFRS, SAP, AutoCAD, HSE certifications

## Documents to keep ready

- Degree certificates, often needing attestation for work visas
- Experience letters
- Passport copy
- Professional licences (engineering, nursing and medical licences have their own exam and registration rules)

## Common job families from India

Construction and engineering, healthcare, hospitality, retail, accounts and finance, sales, logistics, IT, facilities management and drivers.

## Safety

Use recruitment agencies registered under India's eMigrate system, verify the employer, and never hand over your passport or pay large fees for a job offer.
