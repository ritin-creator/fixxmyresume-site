## For freshers

```
SUMMARY
Graduate seeking a customer support role (voice or chat). Fluent in English, Hindi and
Marathi; comfortable with rotational and night shifts; typing speed 40 words a minute.

EXPERIENCE (if any)
Part-time Sales Associate, electronics store | 2025
• Helped 60+ walk-in customers a day choose phones and accessories
```

Add any customer-facing experience: retail, tutoring, events, family business.

## For experienced agents (fictional bullets)

- Handled 80+ inbound calls a day for a telecom process with a 91% CSAT
- Scored 95%+ in quality audits for 10 consecutive months
- Reduced average handle time from 6.5 to 5.2 minutes
- Resolved 120+ chats a day for an e-commerce returns process
- Promoted to subject-matter expert; trained 15 new joiners on refunds

## What to include

- Process type and domain
- Languages (with fluency)
- Shift flexibility
- Typing speed (non-voice)
- Tools: CRM, ticketing (Freshdesk, Zendesk, Salesforce), dialers
- Metrics: CSAT, quality score, AHT, first-call resolution, attendance

Add coaching, quality calibration, rostering, attrition and team metrics. See [customer service resume example](/articles/customer-service-resume-example/).
