## Hard skills vs soft skills

**Hard skills** are teachable and testable: Excel, Python, GST filing, Google Ads, CAD, forklift operation, a language. Recruiters search for these by name.

**Soft skills** are how you work: communication, leadership, problem-solving. Everyone claims them, so they count for more when your bullets prove them ("Led a team of 6" proves leadership better than the word "leadership").

## How to choose

1. Copy the job description into a document.
2. Highlight every tool, method, certification and skill it names.
3. Keep the ones you genuinely have, using the employer's exact words.
4. Add two or three related skills that strengthen your fit.

More on this in [resume keywords](/articles/resume-keywords-from-job-description/).

## Hard skills by field

**Software and IT:** Python, Java, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Node.js, SQL, Git, Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, REST APIs, CI/CD, Linux, system design, unit testing.

**Data and analytics:** SQL, Excel (pivot tables, XLOOKUP), Power BI, Tableau, Python (pandas), R, statistics, A/B testing, data cleaning, forecasting, Looker, BigQuery.

**Finance and accounting:** financial reporting, month-end close, reconciliation, GST, TDS, IFRS, Ind AS, budgeting, forecasting, Tally, SAP FICO, Oracle NetSuite, Zoho Books, audit, financial modelling.

**Marketing:** SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, content strategy, email marketing, marketing automation, Google Analytics 4, copywriting, social media management, campaign reporting, CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce).

**Sales:** lead generation, prospecting, cold calling, negotiation, account management, CRM, pipeline management, distributor management, territory planning, key account management.

**Design:** Figma, Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, user research, wireframing, prototyping, design systems, accessibility, typography.

**Operations and supply chain:** inventory management, procurement, vendor management, logistics, warehouse management systems, Lean, Six Sigma, demand planning, SAP MM.

**HR:** recruitment, onboarding, payroll, HRIS (Workday, Darwinbox, Keka), employee relations, performance management, labour law compliance.

**Customer service:** ticketing systems (Zendesk, Freshdesk), live chat, complaint resolution, CRM, call handling, SLA management, escalation handling.

**Healthcare:** patient care, BLS/ACLS, electronic medical records, medication administration, infection control, triage.

**Education:** lesson planning, curriculum design, classroom management, assessment design, LMS (Moodle, Google Classroom), differentiated instruction.

## Soft skills worth showing (not just listing)

Communication, leadership, teamwork, problem-solving, time management, adaptability, attention to detail, stakeholder management, negotiation, mentoring, critical thinking, ownership, customer focus, conflict resolution.

Turn them into proof:

| Instead of listing | Show it like this |
| --- | --- |
| Leadership | Led a 6-person team through a CRM migration, finishing two weeks early |
| Communication | Presented monthly results to the regional director and 12 branch heads |
| Problem-solving | Traced repeated billing errors to a tax setting and fixed it, ending 200 monthly complaints |

## How to format your skills section

Keep it simple and ATS-friendly:

- **Analytics:** SQL, Python (pandas), Power BI, Excel
- **Tools:** Jira, Confluence, Git
- **Languages:** English (fluent), Hindi (native)

No tables, no bars, no stars.
