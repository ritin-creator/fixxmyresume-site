Numbers do two things on a resume: they make your work concrete, and they let a reader compare you with other candidates. "Handled customer complaints" could mean 3 a month or 300 a day.

## Seven kinds of numbers to look for

1. **Volume:** how many? (calls, orders, clients, students, invoices, deliveries)
2. **Money:** how much? (revenue, budget, savings, deal size)
3. **Time:** how fast or how much saved? (turnaround, hours saved, days cut)
4. **Percentage change:** how much better? (growth, reduction, conversion)
5. **Scale:** how big? (team size, territory, users, locations)
6. **Frequency:** how often? (daily, weekly reports, monthly audits)
7. **Ranking:** how did you compare? (top 5 of 80, first in region)

## A simple formula

**Verb + what you did + number + result.**

> Reduced invoice processing time from 5 days to 2 by moving approvals to a shared tracker.

## Where to find your numbers

- Old performance reviews and appraisal forms
- Monthly reports or dashboards you sent
- Targets and incentive statements
- Emails congratulating you on a result
- Your own rough count ("about how many per day, times working days")

## Estimating honestly

Use "about", "over", "up to" or a range. Make sure you could explain it in an interview: "I handled roughly 40 calls a day, five days a week, so about 800 a month."

What you must not do is make up a number. Recruiters ask about the specific ones, and a made-up figure falls apart under one follow-up question.

## Jobs that don't seem measurable

| Role | Duty | Quantified |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receptionist | Answered phones and greeted visitors | Managed a front desk handling 80+ calls and 40 visitors a day |
| Teacher | Taught mathematics | Taught maths to 4 sections of 40 students; class average rose from 61% to 72% in a year |
| Admin assistant | Organised meetings | Scheduled 25+ meetings a week for 3 directors with no double bookings |
| Nurse | Cared for patients | Cared for 6–8 patients per shift on a 30-bed surgical ward |
| Developer | Fixed bugs | Closed 140 bugs in 6 months, including 12 critical payment issues |
| Delivery executive | Delivered parcels | Completed 9,000+ deliveries over 3 years with a 4.9 customer rating |

## Not every bullet needs a number

Aim for most bullets, not all. A bullet about a qualitative win ("Chosen to train new joiners on the billing system") can be strong without one.
