## Bullet formula

**Problem → what you led → measurable outcome.**

## Sample bullets (fictional)

- Led redesign of onboarding for a fintech app (2M users), raising day-7 activation from 31% to 42%
- Defined and launched UPI autopay for subscriptions, adding ₹3.5 crore in annual recurring revenue within 6 months
- Ran 25 customer interviews that reshaped the roadmap; cut a planned feature saving 2 engineer-quarters
- Worked with 3 engineering teams and design to ship 14 releases in a year with a 99.9% crash-free rate
- Introduced a weekly metrics review that cut decision time on experiments from 3 weeks to 1

## Showing your role honestly

Products are built by teams. Use "led", "defined", "prioritised" for what you owned and "worked with" for shared work. Interviewers will ask exactly what you did.

## Skills to list

Product discovery, user research, roadmapping, prioritisation (RICE, impact vs effort), A/B testing, SQL, analytics tools (Mixpanel, Amplitude, GA4), PRDs, Jira, stakeholder management.

## Aspiring PMs

Coming from engineering, sales, design or consulting? Lead with the product-like work you've done:

- Engineers: shaped requirements, made trade-offs, spoke to users
- Sales and support: turned customer feedback into shipped features
- Consultants: framed problems and drove decisions with data

Add a side project you shipped and its numbers. See [career change resume](/articles/career-change-resume/).
