faq:
- q: What are action verbs on a resume?
  a: Action verbs are the first word of a bullet point that says what you did, such as led, built, reduced or negotiated. They make each line about your contribution rather than your duties.
- q: Should every bullet start with an action verb?
  a: Yes, as a rule. Starting each bullet with a verb keeps the resume short and scannable. Use past tense for past roles and present tense for your current one.
- q: Which action verbs should I avoid?
  a: Weak or vague verbs such as 'helped', 'worked on', 'handled' and 'responsible for'. Replace them with a verb that names the actual action, then add the result.

A verb at the start of each bullet makes it clear what you did and pushes you towards a result. "Responsible for" and "worked on" do neither.

## Leading people

Led, managed, directed, supervised, mentored, coached, trained, hired, onboarded, delegated, motivated, guided, oversaw, headed, chaired, coordinated.

## Building and creating

Built, created, designed, developed, launched, established, founded, introduced, set up, wrote, produced, engineered, implemented, composed, drafted, pioneered.

## Improving

Improved, increased, reduced, cut, streamlined, simplified, upgraded, optimised, automated, accelerated, strengthened, revamped, redesigned, standardised, modernised, refined.

## Growing numbers

Grew, expanded, boosted, doubled, tripled, raised, generated, scaled, lifted, multiplied, won, secured, captured, attracted.

## Saving money and time

Saved, reduced, cut, lowered, consolidated, renegotiated, eliminated, recovered, conserved, trimmed, avoided.

## Selling and persuading

Sold, negotiated, closed, converted, persuaded, pitched, influenced, convinced, upsold, cross-sold, prospected, partnered.

## Analysing and researching

Analysed, assessed, audited, calculated, evaluated, forecast, investigated, measured, modelled, researched, surveyed, tested, tracked, identified, mapped, benchmarked.

## Organising and operating

Organised, planned, scheduled, coordinated, administered, processed, maintained, executed, delivered, dispatched, catalogued, prioritised, allocated.

## Communicating

Presented, wrote, edited, published, briefed, reported, documented, translated, explained, facilitated, moderated, publicised, advocated.

## Helping customers

Resolved, assisted, supported, served, advised, answered, handled, retained, guided, onboarded, consulted, educated.

## Fixing problems

Solved, fixed, debugged, diagnosed, resolved, troubleshot, rescued, restored, repaired, prevented, mitigated.

## Achieving

Achieved, attained, completed, earned, exceeded, outperformed, ranked, surpassed, topped, awarded.

## Before and after

| Weak start | Strong start |
| --- | --- |
| Responsible for the sales team | Led a 9-person sales team that hit 112% of its annual target |
| Worked on reducing costs | Cut packaging costs by 18% by renegotiating with 3 suppliers |
| Helped with onboarding | Onboarded 40 new hires in one quarter using a new onboarding checklist |
| Was involved in a website project | Rebuilt the checkout page, lifting completed orders by 9% |
| Handled customer calls | Resolved 50+ customer calls a day with a 92% satisfaction score |

## Three rules

1. **Past tense** for past jobs, present tense for your current one.
2. **Vary your verbs.** Five bullets starting with "Managed" reads flat.
3. **Pair the verb with a number.** Verbs show action; numbers show scale. See [how to quantify achievements](/articles/how-to-quantify-achievements-on-resume/).
