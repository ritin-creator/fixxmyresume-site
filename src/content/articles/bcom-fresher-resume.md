## What recruiters check for accounts roles

1. Tally (most small and mid-size firms) or SAP/Zoho Books for larger firms
2. GST and TDS basics
3. Excel
4. Practical experience, even an internship or helping in a family business

## No internship?

Show practice another way: maintained accounts for a family shop, completed a GST filing course with practice returns, or volunteered as treasurer of a college society with a budget.
