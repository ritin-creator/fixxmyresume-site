## Structure

1. **Name and contact details** (city, phone, email, LinkedIn)
2. **Summary:** title, total years, domain, one or two headline results
3. **Key skills:** 8–15 terms matching the job
4. **Work experience:** reverse-chronological; 4–6 bullets for recent roles, fewer for older
5. **Education:** degree, institution, year (skip 10th and 12th once you have 5+ years)
6. **Certifications**
7. **Awards** (if notable)

## Length

| Experience | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| 2–7 years | 1 (2 if dense and relevant) |
| 8–15 years | 2 |
| 15+ years | 2, with older roles condensed |

## Showing progression in one company

Indian professionals often grow inside one employer. Show each title separately:

```
Northwind Pharma Ltd, Mumbai | 2016 – Present
Area Sales Manager | Apr 2022 – Present
• Led 9 medical representatives across 4 districts; grew territory sales 21% in two years
Senior Medical Representative | Apr 2019 – Mar 2022
• Ranked top 3 of 60 reps nationally in 2021
Medical Representative | Jun 2016 – Mar 2019
```

## Summary example

> Finance controller with 14 years in FMCG and retail. Led SAP S/4HANA rollout across 3 plants, cut month-end close from 10 to 5 days and manage a team of 12.

## CTC and notice period

Recruiters will ask, but the resume isn't the best place. Put them in the application form or your email. See [CTC and expected CTC](/articles/ctc-and-expected-ctc-on-resume/) and [notice period](/articles/notice-period-on-resume/).

## What to cut

- Responsibilities that anyone in the role would have
- School marks (after about 5 years)
- Personal details: DOB, marital status, father's name, religion
- "Declaration" and signature

## Recruiter scan order

Current title and company → total experience → domain and skills → most recent achievements → education. Make sure each is easy to find in the top half of page one.
