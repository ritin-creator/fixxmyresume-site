There are three classic resume formats. The difference is what comes first: your job history or your skills.

## At a glance

| | Reverse-chronological | Hybrid (combination) | Functional |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What leads | Most recent job | Skills or key achievements, then job history | Skills grouped by theme |
| Shows dates and employers | Yes, clearly | Yes | Often hidden or minimal |
| ATS parsing | Best | Good if single column | Weak |
| Recruiter trust | High | High | Low |
| Best for | Most people with steady work history | Career changers, people with gaps, varied freelancers | Rarely the right choice |

## Reverse-chronological

Your experience is listed from newest to oldest, each with title, company, location, dates and achievement bullets.

**Use it if** you have a steady work history and want a job similar to your current or last one. That covers most applicants.

**Why it works:** recruiters scan for your latest title and employer first, and ATS can calculate your total experience from clean dates.

## Hybrid (combination)

Starts with a summary and a short "Key skills" or "Selected achievements" block, then a full reverse-chronological work history.

**Use it if** your most relevant experience isn't your most recent job, you're [changing careers](/articles/career-change-resume/), or you have a [gap](/articles/employment-gap-on-resume/) and want strengths to show first.

**Keep it ATS-safe:** the skills block should be a simple list, not a table or sidebar, and the work history must still have dates.

## Functional

Groups your experience into skill themes ("Leadership", "Sales") and lists employers briefly at the end, often without dates.

**Why it's risky:** ATS may not map your experience to jobs and dates, so your years of experience can come out as zero. Recruiters know the format is often used to hide gaps or job-hopping. A hybrid gets you the same benefit without those costs.

## What about freshers?

A fresher has little work history, so a chronological resume can look empty. Lead with education, projects and internships, then any work experience. That's still a chronological format inside each section. See [resume format for freshers](/articles/resume-format-for-freshers-india/) and [resume with no experience](/articles/resume-with-no-experience/).

## The bottom line

Default to reverse-chronological. Switch to hybrid when your best story isn't your latest job. Skip functional.
