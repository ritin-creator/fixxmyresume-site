## Summary examples

- **Newly qualified:** "Chartered Accountant (qualified Nov 2025) with 2 years of articleship in statutory audit and direct tax at a mid-size firm; audited 18 clients across manufacturing and services. Seeking an FP&A or controllership role in industry."
- **Experienced:** "Chartered Accountant with 9 years across Big Four audit and FMCG controllership. Led an SAP S/4HANA finance rollout across 3 plants and cut month-end close from 9 days to 5."

## Bullets by area (fictional)

**Statutory and internal audit**
- Led statutory audits for 12 clients with turnover from ₹50 crore to ₹1,200 crore under Ind AS
- Identified ₹2.3 crore of revenue cut-off errors in a manufacturing client; corrected before sign-off

**Direct and indirect tax**
- Managed GST compliance for 9 GSTINs across 7 states; resolved 14 notices without penalty
- Prepared transfer pricing documentation for 3 group companies

**FP&A**
- Built a rolling 12-month forecast model for a ₹900 crore business; forecast accuracy within 3%
- Ran monthly variance analysis for 6 business units with the leadership team

**Controllership**
- Cut month-end close from 9 to 5 working days by standardising accruals and reconciliations
- Owned consolidation of 4 entities and two acquisitions

## Articleship and ranks

For newly qualified CAs, articleship is your experience. Write it as a job with clients, sectors and outcomes. An all-India rank goes in the summary and education.

## Systems and standards

SAP FICO / S/4HANA, Oracle, Tally Prime, Zoho Books, NetSuite, Excel (advanced), Power BI; Ind AS, IFRS, Companies Act, Income Tax Act, GST.
