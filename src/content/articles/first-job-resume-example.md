Below is a complete example for a fictional graduate applying for an entry-level marketing role, followed by a blank template.

## The example

```
ANANYA ROY
Kolkata, India | ananya.roy@email.com | +91 98300 00000 | linkedin.com/in/ananyaroy

SUMMARY
Marketing graduate seeking an entry-level digital marketing role. Ran Instagram ads
for a local café, growing followers from 800 to 5,000 in four months.

EDUCATION
BBA in Marketing, St. Xavier's College, Kolkata | 2023 – 2026 | CGPA 8.4/10
Relevant coursework: Consumer Behaviour, Digital Marketing, Marketing Analytics

EXPERIENCE
Social Media Intern, Bean There Café, Kolkata | Jan 2026 – Apr 2026
• Planned and posted 60+ Instagram posts and reels in four months
• Ran ₹15,000 in Meta ads, bringing 320 coupon redemptions in store
• Grew followers from 800 to 5,000 and doubled weekend footfall on promo days

Volunteer, Kolkata Literary Meet | Jan 2025
• Coordinated a 6-person volunteer desk handling registrations for 1,200 guests

PROJECTS
Marketing analytics case study (coursework)
• Analysed 2,000 survey responses in Excel to segment café customers into 4 groups

SKILLS
Meta Ads Manager, Google Analytics 4, Canva, Excel (pivot tables), copywriting
Languages: English, Bengali, Hindi
```

## Why it works

- **The summary names the target role** and gives one result straight away.
- **The internship reads like a job**, with numbers for output (60+ posts), spend (₹15,000) and outcome (320 redemptions).
- **Volunteering and coursework fill the page** without padding.
- **Skills are tools** that appear in entry-level marketing job ads.
- **Plain text, one column** so ATS can read it.

```
[Degree], [College] | [Years] | [Grade if strong]

EXPERIENCE
[Role], [Organisation], [City] | [Start] – [End]
• [Verb] [what you did] [number] [result]
• [Verb] [what you did] [number] [result]

PROJECTS
[Project name]
• [What you built or analysed, tools used, result]

SKILLS
[Tools and skills from the job description]
Languages: [Languages]
```
