## Format

```
B.Tech, Electrical Engineering | NIT Calicut | 2022 – 2026 | CGPA 8.2/10
Class XII (CBSE) | 2022 | 89%
Class X (CBSE) | 2020 | 9.4 CGPA
```

Always show the maximum (8.2/10, not just 8.2).

## When to convert

- The application form asks for a percentage
- The employer's cut-off is in percentage

Then use your university's official formula and add "(converted)". Different universities use different formulas, so don't use a generic one.

## Semester grades

Use your cumulative CGPA. Add semester SGPA only if it shows a clear upward trend you want to point out.

## How long to keep school marks

| Experience | Include X and XII? |
| --- | --- |
| Fresher, 0–2 years | Yes |
| 3–5 years | Optional |
| 5+ years | No |

## Backlogs

Don't list them on the resume unless a form asks. If asked, answer accurately; background checks verify grade cards.
