- q: What should a customer service resume include?
  a: The channels you handled (phone, chat, email), volume per day, quality or CSAT scores, systems you used such as a CRM or ticketing tool, and languages you speak.
- q: What skills matter most for customer service jobs?
  a: Clear communication, patience, problem solving, typing speed for chat roles, and familiarity with CRM or helpdesk software. Show them with numbers where you can.
- q: How do I write customer service experience with no numbers?
  a: 'Use whatever you can measure: tickets per day, average response time, escalations reduced, or positive feedback received. Approximate honestly, for example ''around 60 calls a day''.'

## Sample bullets (fictional)

- Handled 70+ inbound calls a day for a telecom provider with a 92% customer satisfaction score
- Resolved 85% of chats at first contact on Freshdesk, above the team average of 74%
- Cut average handle time from 7.5 to 5.8 minutes by building a quick-reply library
- Managed escalations for 3 enterprise clients, reducing repeat complaints by about a third
- Trained 12 new joiners on the billing system and refund policy
- Supported customers in English, Hindi and Telugu

## Metrics worth mentioning

| Metric | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Calls, chats or tickets per day | Capacity |
| CSAT or NPS | Quality |
| First-contact resolution | Problem-solving |
| Average handle time | Efficiency |
| Quality audit score | Accuracy and compliance |
| Attendance or schedule adherence | Reliability |

## Skills

Zendesk, Freshdesk, Salesforce Service Cloud, CRM, ticketing, live chat, email support, complaint resolution, de-escalation, typing speed (state it if you know it), languages.
