## Quick comparison

| | PDF | Word (.docx) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Looks the same everywhere | Yes | Not always (fonts, spacing can shift) |
| ATS reading | Good, if text-based | Very good |
| Recruiters can edit | No | Yes (agencies sometimes need this) |
| Best for | Most applications and emails | When requested; some older portals |

## How to check your PDF is text-based

Open it and try to select a line of text. If you can highlight individual words, it's text. If the whole page selects like a picture, it's an image, and an ATS will see nothing.

Export from Word, Google Docs or your resume tool using "Save as PDF" or "Download as PDF". Don't print and scan.

## When to send Word

- The advert or recruiter asks for .doc or .docx.
- A recruitment agency needs to add its logo or remove your contact details.
- A portal's preview shows your PDF jumbled.

## File naming

`Firstname-Lastname-Resume.pdf`. Add the role if you're sending several: `Firstname-Lastname-Data-Analyst.pdf`.
