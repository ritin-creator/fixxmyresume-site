| Country or region | Photo? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| United States | No | Not expected; employers avoid photos to reduce discrimination risk |
| Canada | No | Same as the US |
| United Kingdom, Ireland | No | Not expected |
| Australia, New Zealand | No | Not expected |
| India | Optional, usually no | Not expected for corporate roles; some sectors and smaller employers still ask |
| Germany, Austria, Switzerland | Common, now optional | Traditionally included; increasingly optional |
| France, Spain, Italy, Belgium | Common | Optional but often included |
| Netherlands, Nordic countries | Optional | Varies by employer |
| UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Gulf | Common | Often expected along with nationality and visa status |
| Japan | Expected | Standard Japanese resume formats include one |
| China, South Korea | Common | Often expected |

## If you include a photo

- Recent, passport-style, plain background
- Professional clothing, neutral expression
- Small, top corner, not inside the text column
- Never a cropped holiday or group photo

## If you're unsure

Follow the job advert. If it doesn't ask, leave it out. No employer rejects a strong resume for a missing photo where photos are optional.
