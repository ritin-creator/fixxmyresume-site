> Applying abroad too? See the general list of [25 Resume Mistakes That Cost You Interviews](/articles/resume-mistakes-to-avoid/).


1. **Father's name, religion, caste, marital status.** Biodata leftovers. *Fix:* remove unless asked. See [personal details](/articles/personal-details-on-indian-resume/).
2. **"Declaration: I hereby declare…" with signature and place.** *Fix:* delete for private jobs.
3. **"To work in a reputed organisation…"** *Fix:* name the role and one proof ([25 objective examples](/articles/resume-objective-for-freshers/)).
4. **Two-column Word or Canva template with a photo.** *Fix:* one column, no photo ([ATS checklist](/articles/how-to-make-resume-ats-friendly/)).
5. **Duties, no numbers.** "Responsible for sales." *Fix:* "Achieved 118% of target across 280 outlets."
6. **Copied skill lists.** Fifteen languages you met once. *Fix:* keep what you can be tested on.
7. **Mixed date formats.** "2019 to 2021", "Jun'22 – till date". *Fix:* "Jun 2022 – Present".
8. **Hobbies that say nothing.** "Watching TV, listening to music." *Fix:* remove, or show something real (state-level chess, running half marathons).
9. **Email like cooldude123@.** *Fix:* firstname.lastname@.
10. **Phone number without a country code for overseas jobs.** *Fix:* +91.
11. **Two pages for a fresher.** *Fix:* one page.
12. **Grammar errors in common words.** "Responsible of", "having knowledge in". *Fix:* short, simple sentences; ask someone to proofread.
13. **Same resume for every job.** *Fix:* [tailor in 15 minutes](/articles/tailor-resume-to-job-description/).
14. **Old file uploaded on Naukri.** *Fix:* update when your role, skills or city changes ([Naukri guide](/articles/naukri-resume-guide/)).
15. **Fake experience or certificates.** Background checks catch them, and offers get withdrawn. *Fix:* present real work well.
