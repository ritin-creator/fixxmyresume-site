The summary sits at the top of your resume and answers the recruiter's first question: "Is this person a fit for this role?"

## The formula

**[Job title] with [X years] in [industry/area], skilled in [2–3 key skills]. [One result with a number].**

Optional third line: what you're looking for next, if you're changing direction.

## Freshers and students

1. Computer science graduate (B.Tech, 2026) skilled in Java, Spring Boot and SQL. Built a college event booking app used by 1,200 students during the annual fest.
2. Commerce graduate with a 6-month internship in accounts payable. Comfortable with Tally, Excel and GST invoicing; processed 300+ vendor invoices with no errors flagged in audit.
3. Marketing graduate with hands-on experience running Instagram ads for a local café, growing followers from 800 to 5,000 in four months.
4. Recent nursing graduate with 1,000+ clinical hours across medical, surgical and paediatric wards, certified in Basic Life Support.
5. Mechanical engineering graduate with an internship in production planning; reduced setup time on one line by 15% through a changeover checklist.

6. Customer support executive with 2 years in e-commerce, handling 60+ chats a day with a 94% satisfaction score.

9. Content writer with 2 years writing SEO articles for B2B SaaS; 15 articles reached Google's first page for target terms.
10. HR executive with 2 years in end-to-end recruitment, closing 70+ positions across sales and operations.

## Specific situations

26. **Returning after a break:** Chartered accountant returning after a 2-year career break, with 6 prior years in statutory audit and recent certification in IFRS.
27. **Freelancer:** Freelance graphic designer with 5 years and 60+ clients across retail and hospitality, specialising in packaging and brand identity.
28. **Remote role:** Customer success manager with 4 years in fully remote teams across three time zones, retaining 96% of a $3M book of accounts.
29. **Gig to full-time:** Delivery partner moving into a warehouse supervisor role, with 3 years and 9,000+ deliveries at a 4.9 rating.
30. **Internal promotion:** Senior analyst applying for team lead, mentoring 3 junior analysts and owning the weekly business review.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Vague adjectives:** "hardworking, passionate, dynamic" with nothing to back them.
- **Writing an objective about you:** "Seeking a challenging role to grow my career" tells the reader what you want, not what you offer.
- **First person:** drop "I am a…".
- **Missing the job title:** recruiters and ATS search by title. Put the target title in the first words.
