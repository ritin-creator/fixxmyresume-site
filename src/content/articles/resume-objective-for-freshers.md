## Formula

**[Degree] graduate seeking a [specific role], [one proof: project, internship, skill or result].**

## Engineering and IT

1. B.Tech (CSE) graduate seeking a backend developer role; built a Spring Boot placement portal used by 900 students.
2. BCA graduate seeking a junior web developer role; built a PHP inventory system for a local pharmacy tracking 1,500 products.
3. MCA graduate seeking a QA engineer role, with Selenium basics and 200+ test cases written during internship.
4. B.Tech (ECE) graduate seeking an embedded systems role; designed an ESP32 soil monitor that cut water use by about 30% in trials.
5. B.Tech (Mechanical) graduate seeking a production engineer role; cut fixture setup time by 15% during an auto-parts internship.
6. Diploma (Electrical) holder seeking a maintenance technician role, with 6 months of apprenticeship on LT panels.

## Commerce and finance

7. B.Com graduate seeking an accounts executive role; 6-month internship with Tally Prime and GST data preparation.
8. B.Com graduate seeking a banking sales role; raised ₹6 lakh in sponsorships as fest sponsorship head.
9. CA Intermediate student seeking articleship in statutory audit, with family-business accounting experience in Tally.
10. BBA (Finance) graduate seeking a financial analyst trainee role, with a valuation project on two listed FMCG companies.

## MBA

11. MBA (Marketing) graduate seeking a brand or trade marketing role; summer project mapped 120 retail outlets for a new product.
12. MBA (HR) graduate seeking an HR generalist role; redesigned onboarding for 200 annual hires during internship.
13. MBA (Operations) graduate seeking a supply chain analyst role; warehouse layout change raised picking rate by 18%.
14. MBA (Finance) graduate seeking a credit analyst role, with 12 MSME loan appraisals during internship.

## Science, arts and others

15. B.Sc (Statistics) graduate seeking a data analyst role, skilled in Excel, SQL and R.
16. BA (English) graduate seeking a content writer role; published 30 articles on a college blog with 10,000 reads.
17. BA (Psychology) graduate seeking an HR or talent acquisition role, with 3 months of recruitment internship.
18. B.Sc Nursing graduate seeking a staff nurse role, with 1,000+ clinical hours and BLS certification.
19. B.Pharm graduate seeking a medical representative role; strong in pharmacology and fluent in Hindi and English.
20. Hotel management graduate seeking a front office role; 6-month industrial training at a 5-star hotel.

## Customer support, sales and operations

21. Graduate seeking a customer support (voice/chat) role; fluent in English, Hindi and Telugu, open to night shifts.
22. Graduate seeking a field sales role in Lucknow; owns a two-wheeler and speaks Hindi and Awadhi.
23. 12th pass candidate seeking a retail sales associate role, with 1 year helping in the family electronics shop.
24. Graduate seeking a data entry role; typing speed 40 words a minute with high accuracy.
25. Graduate seeking a logistics coordinator role; managed event logistics for a 3,000-person college fest.

## Avoid

> "To work in a reputed organisation where I can utilise my skills and grow with the company."

It says nothing specific. Compare it with any line above.
