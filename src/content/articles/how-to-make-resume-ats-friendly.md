> New to ATS? Start with [What Is an ATS Resume?](/articles/what-is-an-ats-resume/). This page is the hands-on checklist. Hinglish mein padhna hai? [ATS Resume Kya Hai](/articles/ats-resume-kya-hai/).


Work through these 15 checks in order. Most resumes fail on two or three of them, and each fix takes minutes.

## Layout

1. **One column.** Side-by-side columns are the most common cause of jumbled parsing. Move sidebars (skills, languages, contact) into the main flow.
2. **No tables or text boxes.** Content inside them is often skipped. Use plain lines and bullets.
3. **Contact details in the body.** Put name, phone, email, city and LinkedIn as text at the top of the page, not inside the document header or footer.
4. **No icons, images, logos or skill bars.** Write "Phone:" instead of a phone icon. Replace "Python ●●●○○" with "Python".
5. **No photo** unless the country or job expects one. See the [country guide](/articles/photo-on-resume-by-country/).

## Text

6. **Standard section headings.** Summary, Experience (or Work Experience), Education, Skills, Projects, Certifications. Creative headings confuse parsers.
7. **Common fonts, 10 to 12 points.** Arial, Calibri, Helvetica, Georgia, Garamond. Avoid decorative or icon fonts.
8. **One date format.** "Jan 2022 – Present" everywhere. Mixed formats can make the system miscount your experience.
9. **Job title, company, dates on clear lines.** For example: `Data Analyst, Acme Retail` then `Pune | Jan 2022 – Present`.
10. **Spell out abbreviations once.** "Search engine optimisation (SEO)". Recruiters may search for either.

## Keywords

11. **Mirror the job description.** Use the exact job title and the tools and skills it names, wherever they honestly describe your work. See [how to find resume keywords](/articles/resume-keywords-from-job-description/).
12. **Put keywords in context.** A skill listed in Skills and shown in a bullet ("Built weekly Power BI dashboards for 40 store managers") counts for more with people and with AI matching.

## File

13. **Text-based PDF or Word.** Never a scanned image. Use Word if the employer asks for it. More in [PDF or Word](/articles/pdf-or-word-resume/).
14. **Clear file name.** `Firstname-Lastname-Resume.pdf`.

## Test

15. **Do the copy-paste test.** Open your PDF, select all, copy, and paste into a plain text editor (Notepad, TextEdit in plain text mode). Read it top to bottom. If sections come out in the wrong order, words are missing, or your contact details vanished, fix the layout.

## Quick before-and-after

| Before | After |
| --- | --- |
| Skills in a left sidebar with rating dots | Skills section after Experience, plain comma list |
| Email in the header area | Email in the first line under your name |
| "Where I've worked" | "Experience" |
| "2019 - 05/2021" and "June 2021 - now" | "Jan 2019 – May 2021" and "Jun 2021 – Present" |
| Profile photo and icons | Removed |

## What ATS-friendly does not mean

It doesn't mean stuffing keywords in white text or repeating them 20 times. Hidden text is easy for recruiters to spot and can get you rejected. It also doesn't guarantee an interview: the content still has to convince a person. That's why the second half of the job is [writing strong bullets](/articles/how-to-quantify-achievements-on-resume/).

## Section headings ATS recognise

Parsers sort your text into fields by looking for familiar headings. Use the plain version on the left; the ones on the right are the kind that get missed or misfiled.

| Use this | Not this |
| --- | --- |
| Summary | About me, My story, Who I am |
| Experience / Work experience | Where I've been, Career journey |
| Education | Academic background, Learning |
| Skills | Toolbox, What I bring, Superpowers |
| Projects | Things I've built |
| Certifications | Badges, Credentials wall |

## Dates and job titles that parse cleanly

Most systems calculate your total experience from the dates you give, so small inconsistencies add up.

- Write month and year for every role: `Mar 2023 – Present`, not `2023 – now` on one line and `03/2023` on another.
- Put dates on the same line as the company or right under it, never in a separate column.
- If you held two titles at one company, list them as two entries with their own dates, or as `Senior Analyst (2024–Present), Analyst (2022–2024)`.
- Use your real job title. If it's unusual (`Ninja, Growth`), add the standard equivalent in brackets: `Growth Ninja (Digital Marketing Executive)`.

## Special cases

**Two-page resumes.** Fine for experienced candidates. Repeat your name in the second page's body text if you want, but don't rely on headers and footers to carry anything important.

**Freshers.** Put Education and Projects above any short internships. Keep marks and CGPA in plain text, for example `CGPA 8.1/10`, not in a table.

**Naukri and LinkedIn uploads.** After uploading, check the fields the portal filled in for you. If your job titles, dates or skills come out wrong, the layout is confusing their parser too. See the [Naukri resume guide](/articles/naukri-resume-guide/).

**Designer resumes.** If you need a visual resume for a portfolio, keep a plain ATS version for online applications and send the designed one by email or link.

## The order to fix things

If you only have twenty minutes, fix in this order, because each step solves more than the next:

1. Move everything into one column and out of tables, text boxes, headers and footers.
2. Rename headings to the standard ones above.
3. Make every date use one format.
4. Add the job title and four or five key terms from the job description where they're true.
5. Export a text-based PDF and run the copy-paste test again.
