All names and companies below are fictional. Adapt the facts to your own.

## 1. Entry level (marketing)

> Dear Ms Kapoor,
>
> I'm applying for the Digital Marketing Associate role at Brightleaf Foods. Your switch to WhatsApp ordering caught my eye because I ran a similar experiment during my internship.
>
> At Bean There Café, I managed Instagram and Meta ads on a ₹15,000 budget, bringing 320 in-store coupon redemptions and growing followers from 800 to 5,000 in four months.
>
> You're looking for someone comfortable with paid social and reporting. I built weekly reports in Google Analytics and Excel for the café owner, and I'd bring the same habit to your team.
>
> I'd be glad to talk. Thank you for considering me.

## 2. Experienced professional (finance)

> Dear Mr Iyer,
>
> I'm applying for the Finance Controller role at Northwind Logistics. With your expansion into three new states, a fast and reliable month-end matters more than ever, and that's been my focus for the last five years.
>
> At Acme Distribution, I cut month-end close from 10 days to 5 and led our move from Tally to SAP across 14 branches without a missed GST filing.
>
> I'd welcome a conversation about your plans for the finance team.

## 3. Career change (teacher to instructional design)

> Dear Hiring Team,
>
> I'm applying for the Instructional Designer role at LearnWell. After seven years teaching science, I've spent the last year building online courses, and I'd like to do it full time.
>
> I designed 40+ lesson plans and assessments a year, and redesigned one unit that lifted average scores by 11%. I've since completed a certificate in Articulate Storyline and built two short courses, linked in my resume.
>
> I'd love to show you how classroom experience can make your courses easier to learn from.

## 4. Internal move

> Hi Rahul,
>
> I'd like to be considered for the Team Lead opening in Analytics. Over the last year I've mentored our three junior analysts and run the weekly business review, and I'd like to take that on formally.
>
> Happy to talk whenever suits you.

## 5. Returning after a gap

> Dear Ms Fernandes,
>
> I'm applying for the Senior Accountant role. I'm returning after a two-year career break for caregiving, and over the last six months I've completed an IFRS certificate to bring my knowledge up to date.
>
> Before my break, I spent six years in statutory audit at a mid-size firm, leading audits for 12 manufacturing clients.
>
> I'd value the chance to discuss the role.

## 6. Cold email to a recruiter

> Subject: Data analyst, 4 years in retail, Pune
>
> Hi Neha, I saw you hire analysts for Northwind. I'm a data analyst with 4 years in retail; my Power BI dashboards cut weekly reporting time by 60% for 40 store managers. Are you hiring for analytics roles this quarter? Resume attached. Thanks, Priya
