Everyone's first resume has this problem, and recruiters hiring for entry-level roles know it. They're not looking for years of experience; they're looking for evidence you can learn, finish things and work with people.

## The layout

1. **Name and contact details**
2. **Summary** (2 lines): target role, degree, one strong thing you've done
3. **Education:** degree, college, year, grade if good, relevant coursework
4. **Projects:** 2–3 with what you built, tools used and a result
5. **Experience:** internships, part-time work, volunteering, freelance
6. **Skills:** tools and methods that match the job
7. **Certifications and achievements** (optional)

## Where to find "experience"

- **Projects:** college projects, personal projects, hackathons, case competitions
- **Internships:** even short or unpaid ones
- **Part-time and gig jobs:** retail, tutoring, delivery, call centre work
- **Volunteering:** NGOs, events, community drives
- **Family business:** what you actually did and what changed
- **Clubs and societies:** organising events, running social media, managing budgets

## Turning them into bullets

| Plain | Strong |
| --- | --- |
| Member of the college cultural committee | Managed a ₹2 lakh budget for the annual fest with 3,000 attendees |
| Did a final-year project on machine learning | Built a crop disease classifier in Python (TensorFlow) with 91% accuracy on 5,000 leaf images |
| Tutored school students | Tutored 6 class 10 students in maths; 5 improved by at least one grade |
| Helped in family shop | Set up WhatsApp ordering for the family store, adding about 40 orders a week |
| Worked part-time at a café | Served 150+ customers per shift and trained 2 new staff on the billing system |

## Summary examples

- "Commerce graduate looking for an accounts trainee role, with a 3-month internship in accounts payable and working knowledge of Tally and Excel."
- "Computer science student skilled in Python and SQL, seeking a data analyst internship; built a sales dashboard for a local retailer."

## Mistakes to avoid

- Long objectives about wanting "a challenging environment"
- Listing school marks and every certificate since class 6
- Two pages of padding
- Photos, date of birth and family details unless the job asks ([India guide](/articles/personal-details-on-indian-resume/))
