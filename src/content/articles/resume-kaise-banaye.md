Resume aapka pehla introduction hai. Recruiter kuch hi second mein decide karta hai ki aage padhna hai ya nahi, aur kai baar pehle software (ATS) resume ko padhta hai. Isliye resume simple, saaf aur specific hona chahiye.

## Step 1: Format choose karo

- **Ek page** (freshers aur 8 saal se kam experience ke liye)
- **Ek column**, do column wale fancy template nahi
- Font: Arial ya Calibri, size 10.5–11
- File: **PDF**, naam jaise `Rahul-Sharma-Resume.pdf`

## Step 2: Contact details

```
RAHUL SHARMA
Lucknow, UP | rahul.sharma@email.com | +91 98000 00000 | linkedin.com/in/rahulsharma
```

Email professional rakho (naam.surname@). Poora ghar ka address zaroori nahi, sirf city kaafi hai.

## Step 3: Summary (2 line)

Batao kaunsi job chahiye aur aapki sabse strong baat kya hai.

> B.Com graduate, accounts executive role ke liye. 6 mahine ki internship mein Tally Prime par 300+ invoices process kiye aur GST data banaya.

"Hardworking aur dedicated insaan, achhi company mein growth chahta hoon" jaisa mat likho. Isse recruiter ko kuch pata nahi chalta.

## Step 4: Experience ya projects

Har point ka formula: **action word + kya kiya + number + result**.

| Kamzor | Strong |
| --- | --- |
| Customer handle kiye | Roz 60+ customer calls handle kiye, 92% satisfaction score |
| Sales ka kaam kiya | Ek saal mein 64 naye retail outlets jode, target ka 118% achieve kiya |
| College project kiya | React aur Node.js se events app banaya, 1,200 students ne use kiya |

Fresher ho to internships, projects, part-time job, family business, NSS/NCC sab yahan aa sakta hai.

## Step 5: Education

```
B.Com, Lucknow University | 2023 – 2026 | 72%
Class XII (UP Board) | 2023 | 81%
Class X (CBSE) | 2021 | 85%
```

## Step 6: Skills

Job description mein jo skills likhi hain, unme se jo aapko sach mein aati hain, wahi likho: Tally Prime, Excel, GST, Java, SQL, Canva, etc. Languages bhi likho: Hindi, English, Bhojpuri.

## Kya NAHI daalna

- Date of birth, father's name, religion, caste, marital status
- Badi photo
- "Declaration: I hereby declare..." aur signature
- "Hobbies: TV dekhna, music sunna"

Ye purane biodata style ki cheezein hain. Details: [biodata vs resume](/articles/biodata-vs-resume-vs-cv/).

## Step 7: Check karo

- [ ] Phone aur email sahi hain?
- [ ] Har point action word se shuru?
- [ ] Zyada tar points mein number hai?
- [ ] Ek page?
- [ ] Spelling galtiyan nahi?
