## Bullets by specialism (fictional numbers)

**Performance marketing**
- Managed ₹50 lakh a month across Google and Meta Ads at a 3.2x return on ad spend
- Cut cost per lead from ₹420 to ₹260 by rebuilding audiences and landing pages
- Set up GA4 conversion tracking and server-side events, recovering about 18% of lost attribution

**SEO and content**
- Grew organic sessions from 40,000 to 160,000 a month in 14 months
- Wrote and edited 120 articles; 22 reached Google's first page for target terms
- Built a content calendar and brief template used by 6 freelance writers

**Brand and product marketing**
- Launched a new snack range in 3 states, reaching 1,800 outlets in the first quarter
- Ran positioning research with 30 customer interviews that shaped the launch message
- Managed a ₹2 crore campaign with 2 agencies, delivering on budget

**Social and community**
- Grew Instagram from 12,000 to 85,000 followers in a year with a weekly reels series
- Built a 3,000-member WhatsApp community driving 15% of repeat orders

## Tools to list (pick what you use)

Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, GA4, Google Tag Manager, Search Console, Semrush or Ahrefs, HubSpot, Mailchimp, Canva, Figma, Looker Studio, Excel.

## Common mistakes

- Vanity metrics only (likes, impressions) with no business result
- "Managed social media" with no scale
- Not stating the budget you handled
