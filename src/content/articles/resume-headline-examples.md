## Headline vs summary

A headline is one line; a summary is two or three sentences. Use both: the headline for scanning, the [summary](/articles/resume-summary-examples/) for the pitch. On Naukri, the "Resume headline" field works the same way; see [Naukri headline and summary](/articles/naukri-resume-headline-and-profile-summary/).
