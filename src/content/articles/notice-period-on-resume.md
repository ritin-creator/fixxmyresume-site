- q: What is a notice period?
  a: The time you must keep working after resigning, as set in your employment contract, commonly 30, 60 or 90 days in India.
- q: Can I negotiate my notice period?
  a: Often yes. Many employers allow a shorter notice by mutual agreement or buyout. Say 'negotiable' only if you have checked your contract and your manager is open to it.

## When to put it on the resume

- **Immediate joiner** or short notice (up to about 30 days): it's an advantage, so say so
- **Serving notice:** "Serving notice; last working day 15 Dec 2026"
- **Recruiter asked** for it on the resume

Place it at the end of the contact line or in a short "Additional information" line:

> Notice period: 30 days (negotiable)

## When to leave it off

- A long notice (60–90 days) that you'll explain in conversation
- When the job portal or form already asks

## Be accurate

Recruiters filter and plan around notice periods. Stating 30 days and then asking for 90 hurts trust. If a buyout is possible, say "90 days, buyout possible" in the conversation, not the resume.
