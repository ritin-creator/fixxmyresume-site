> Applying abroad, or with no projects yet? See [Resume With No Work Experience](/articles/resume-with-no-experience/).


India adds millions of graduates to the job market every year, and fresher job posts on portals get hundreds of applications. Recruiters spend seconds on each resume, and much of the first sorting happens in software. A clean, specific one-page resume is the quickest way to stand out.

## The order

1. Name and contact details
2. Summary (2 lines)
3. Education
4. Projects
5. Internships and experience
6. Skills
7. Certifications and achievements
8. Extracurriculars (only if strong)

For IT freshers, many recruiters prefer **Skills** right after the summary, because they search by technology. Either order is fine if the layout is clean.

## 1. Name and contact details

```
RAHUL SHARMA
Jaipur, Rajasthan | rahul.sharma@email.com | +91 98290 00000 | linkedin.com/in/rahulsharma | github.com/rahuls
```

Use a professional email (firstname.lastname). Put details in the body, not the Word header.

## 2. Summary

Name the role, your degree and your strongest proof:

> B.Tech (Computer Science) 2026 graduate seeking a software developer role. Built a college events app in React and Node.js used by 1,200 students; skilled in Java, SQL and Git.

Skip "To work in a reputed organisation that offers growth". See [objectives for freshers](/articles/resume-objective-for-freshers/).

## 3. Education

```
B.Tech, Computer Science | Poornima College of Engineering, Jaipur | 2022 – 2026 | CGPA 8.1/10
Class XII (CBSE) | Kendriya Vidyalaya, Jaipur | 2022 | 88%
Class X (CBSE) | Kendriya Vidyalaya, Jaipur | 2020 | 91%
```

Write the scale your university uses. More on [CGPA and percentage](/articles/cgpa-and-percentage-on-resume/).

## 4. Projects

Two or three, each with what you built, tools used and a result. This is where freshers win or lose. See [writing the project section](/articles/project-section-in-fresher-resume/).

> **Campus Events App** | React, Node.js, MongoDB
> • Built a booking app for 40 college events; 1,200 students registered in the first week
> • Added QR check-in that cut entry queues from about 20 minutes to 5

## 5. Internships and experience

Even short or unpaid internships count. Write them like jobs:

> **Data Analyst Intern**, Northwind Retail, Jaipur | May 2025 – Jul 2025
> • Cleaned 3 years of sales data in Excel and built a stock-out report used by 6 store managers

Part-time work, tutoring, family business and NSS or NCC roles can go here too.

## 6. Skills

Match the job description. Group them:

- **Programming:** Java, Python, SQL
- **Web:** React, Node.js, HTML, CSS
- **Tools:** Git, VS Code, Postman
- **Languages:** English, Hindi

Full list: [skills for a fresher resume](/articles/skills-for-fresher-resume-india/).

## 7. Certifications and achievements

NPTEL, Coursera, AWS, Google certificates; hackathon wins; scholarships; ranks. Keep the ones relevant to the job.

## What to leave out

| Leave out | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Date of birth, age | Not needed; invites age bias |
| Father's name, mother's name | Not relevant |
| Religion, caste, marital status | Not relevant; can invite bias |
| Photo | Not expected for most private jobs |
| Full address with house number | City is enough |
| Declaration and signature | Outdated for private-sector jobs |
| Hobbies like "listening to music" | Uses space without telling anything |

More on this: [personal details on an Indian resume](/articles/personal-details-on-indian-resume/).

## Format by degree

| Degree | Lead with |
| --- | --- |
| B.Tech, BCA, MCA | Skills and projects |
| B.Com, BBA | Internships, Tally/Excel/GST skills, certifications |
| MBA | Internships (summer internship project), case competitions, specialisation |
| B.Sc, BA | Projects, internships, relevant coursework |
| Diploma, ITI | Practical training, apprenticeships, tools and machines handled |

Examples: [B.Tech](/articles/btech-fresher-resume/), [MBA](/articles/mba-fresher-resume/), [B.Com](/articles/bcom-fresher-resume/), [BCA/MCA](/articles/bca-mca-fresher-resume/).

## A complete one-page sample (B.Tech CSE fresher)

```
ANANYA IYER
Chennai | ananya.iyer@email.com | +91 98XXX XXXXX | linkedin.com/in/ananyaiyer | github.com/ananyaiyer

SUMMARY
Final-year B.Tech CSE student targeting backend developer roles. Built a Spring Boot
attendance system used by 600 students; 2-month internship on REST APIs.

EDUCATION
B.Tech, Computer Science, SRM Institute of Science and Technology | 2022 – 2026 | CGPA 8.4/10
Class XII (CBSE) | 2022 | 91%     Class X (CBSE) | 2020 | 94%

PROJECTS
Smart Attendance System | Java, Spring Boot, MySQL
- QR-based attendance for 600 students across 12 classes; cut roll-call time by ~10 minutes per lecture
Expense Splitter | React, Node.js, MongoDB
- Group expense app with settle-up suggestions; 150 users from campus in the first month

INTERNSHIP
Backend Intern, Finlytics Pvt Ltd, Chennai | May 2025 – Jul 2025
- Built 4 REST endpoints for a loan-status service; wrote unit tests reaching 85% coverage

SKILLS
Languages: Java, Python, JavaScript   Frameworks: Spring Boot, React, Node.js
Databases: MySQL, MongoDB   Tools: Git, Postman, Docker (basics)

ACHIEVEMENTS AND ACTIVITIES
- CodeChef 3-star (max rating 1720)
- Technical lead, college coding club; organised 2 hackathons with 300+ participants
```

The structure is the point here: summary, education with marks, projects with tools and results, internship, grouped skills, then achievements. Replace every detail with your own.

## 60-second check before you send

- Name, phone and email correct, and the email is professional
- One page, one column, text-based PDF
- CGPA or percentage shown the same way throughout
- Every project and internship bullet starts with a verb and has a number where possible
- Skills match the job description and you can explain each one
- No date of birth, father's name, photo or declaration (unless asked)
- File named `Firstname-Lastname-Resume.pdf`

## Before you upload to Naukri or apply

- [ ] One page, one column
- [ ] Text-based PDF named `Firstname-Lastname-Resume.pdf`
- [ ] Target role in the summary
- [ ] Every project has a tool list and a result
- [ ] No spelling mistakes in your email or phone
