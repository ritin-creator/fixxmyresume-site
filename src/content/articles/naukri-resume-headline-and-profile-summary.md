faq:
- q: How long can a Naukri resume headline be?
  a: 'Naukri gives a limited character count, so keep it to one line: role, years of experience and two or three key skills.'
- q: Should my Naukri headline and resume summary be the same?
  a: Similar, not identical. Use the same core keywords, but let the profile summary add context and one or two achievements.
- q: How often should I update my Naukri profile?
  a: Small, genuine updates every few weeks keep the profile fresh in recruiter searches. Change something real, such as a skill or a recent achievement.

## Profile summary examples

**16. Fresher, IT**
> B.Tech Computer Science 2026 graduate with internships in backend development. Built a REST API in Spring Boot for a college placement portal used by 900 students. Strong in Java, SQL and Git, and looking for a software developer role.

**17. Fresher, commerce**
> B.Com graduate with a 6-month accounts internship. Processed 300+ purchase invoices in Tally Prime, prepared GSTR-1 data and reconciled two bank accounts monthly. Seeking an accounts executive role.

**18. Accounts executive**
> Accounts executive with 3 years in a distribution company. Handle accounts payable for 120 vendors, monthly GST returns and bank reconciliation in Tally Prime. Reduced vendor payment delays by setting up a weekly payment run.

**19. Software developer**
> Java developer with 4 years building microservices for e-commerce. Rebuilt the order service in Spring Boot, cutting response times by 40%, and moved 5 services to AWS. Interested in backend roles in product companies.

**20. Field sales**
> FMCG field sales executive with 5 years in Uttar Pradesh general trade. Achieved over 110% of target three years running and added 85 new outlets in 2025. Strong distributor and retailer relationships.

**21. HR recruiter**
> Recruiter with 3 years in IT and non-IT hiring for a staffing firm. Closed 220+ positions, including bulk drives of 50+ hires for BPO clients. Comfortable with Naukri RMS, LinkedIn Recruiter and screening calls in Hindi and English.

**22. Chartered Accountant**
> Chartered Accountant with 6 years in statutory audit at a mid-size firm. Led audits for 15 manufacturing and services clients under Ind AS; trained 10 article assistants.

**23. Digital marketing**
> Performance marketer with 6 years in D2C brands, managing ₹50 lakh monthly ad spend at a 3x return. Strong in Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4 and landing page testing.

**24. Operations**
> Operations manager with 10 years in third-party logistics. Run a 60,000 sq ft warehouse with 70 staff at 99.5% order accuracy.

**25. Returning after a break**
> Accountant returning after a 2-year career break, with 5 prior years in accounts and taxation. Recently completed a GST practitioner course and refreshed Tally Prime skills.
