Remote employers worry about two things: will you get work done without someone watching, and can you communicate in writing across time zones? Your resume should answer both.

## Show remote experience clearly

Put it in the location line:

```
Customer Success Manager, Northwind SaaS | Remote (India, team across UK and US) | 2022 – Present
```

## Add bullets that prove remote skills

- Worked across IST, GMT and EST with a 3-hour daily overlap; ran weekly async updates in Notion for 14 stakeholders
- Wrote 40+ help-centre articles that cut repeat support tickets by 20%
- Delivered a 6-month integration project fully remotely with a team in 3 countries
- Kept a 98% on-time delivery record across 120 freelance projects

## Tools to list

Slack, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Google Workspace, Notion, Confluence, Jira, Asana, Trello, Loom, GitHub, Figma, time-tracking tools if relevant.

## Contact line

Add your time zone and working-hour flexibility if the job is international: "Pune, India (IST, available until 11 pm for US overlap)".

## No remote experience yet?

Use freelance work, online courses with deadlines, open-source contributions, or projects with people in other cities. Show that you planned your own work and finished it.
