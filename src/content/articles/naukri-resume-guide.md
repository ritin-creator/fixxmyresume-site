Naukri works two ways: you apply to jobs, and recruiters search its resume database for candidates. The second is where most experienced hires come from, and it depends on your **profile fields**, not just the PDF.

## 1. Upload a resume that parses cleanly

Use one column, standard headings and contact details in the body. Check the auto-filled fields after upload: if your job titles or dates are wrong, the layout is confusing the parser. See the [ATS checklist](/articles/how-to-make-resume-ats-friendly/).

## 2. Write a specific resume headline

Recruiters see it in search results. Make it role + experience + skills:

> Data Analyst with 4 years in retail | SQL, Power BI, Python | Pune

Not: "Seeking a challenging job". Examples: [Naukri headline and profile summary](/articles/naukri-resume-headline-and-profile-summary/).

## 3. Fill key skills carefully

Recruiters filter by skills. Add 10–15, using the common names recruiters type: "Tally ERP", "GST", "Power BI", "Java", "Spring Boot", "B2B Sales", "Key Account Management". Remove skills you no longer use.

## 4. Employment details

For each job, add designation, company, dates and a short description with two or three achievements. Keep titles and dates identical to your resume and LinkedIn.

## 5. Profile summary

Three to five lines on your experience, domain, top skills and best results. This is your pitch to a recruiter who opened your profile.

## 6. Location, notice period and salary

- **Current and preferred locations:** recruiters filter by city.
- **Notice period:** be accurate; many searches filter by it.
- **Current and expected salary:** fill these honestly; you can choose visibility settings.

## 7. Keep it active

Update your profile now and then: a new skill, a project, a certification. Respond to recruiter messages; it keeps your profile active.

## 8. Apply smart

- Read the job title and requirements; apply where you match most of them
- Tailor the uploaded resume for big applications ([15-minute method](/articles/tailor-resume-to-job-description/))
- Answer screening questions accurately

## How recruiters actually find you on Naukri

When a recruiter searches Naukri's resume database, they type keywords (job titles and skills) and narrow the list with filters such as experience, current location, notice period, salary and how recently a profile was active. Your profile appears only if it matches the keywords and passes the filters, so:

- **Keywords come from your headline, key skills, job titles and profile summary.** Use the words a recruiter would type, not internal company jargon.
- **Filters come from your profile fields.** A wrong notice period or an outdated location quietly removes you from searches you'd be right for.
- **Recency matters.** Recruiters often sort or filter by recently active profiles, which is why genuine updates help.

## Headline and summary examples by role

**Accounts executive (3 years):**

> Headline: Accounts Executive | 3 yrs | GST, TDS, Tally Prime, Bank Reconciliation | Delhi NCR
>
> Summary: Accounts executive with 3 years in a manufacturing company handling GST returns, TDS, vendor payments and month-end closing in Tally Prime. Cut reconciliation time from 4 days to 2 by building Excel trackers. Looking for senior accounts roles in Delhi NCR.

**Inside sales (2 years):**

> Headline: Inside Sales Executive | 2 yrs EdTech | B2C Sales, CRM, Lead Conversion | Bengaluru
>
> Summary: Inside sales executive with 2 years selling online courses by phone and WhatsApp. Converted 9–11% of qualified leads against a team average of 7%, and was a top-3 performer for 5 months of 2025. Comfortable with LeadSquared and targets-driven work.

**Java developer (4 years):**

> Headline: Java Developer | 4 yrs | Spring Boot, Microservices, MySQL, AWS | Pune
>
> Summary: Backend developer with 4 years building Spring Boot microservices for a fintech platform handling about 2 lakh transactions a day. Led migration of two services to AWS and improved API response times by 30%.

Use these as patterns. Every number and claim must be your own.

## Which fields to fill first

If your profile is half-empty, fill these in this order. They have the biggest effect on whether you show up in searches:

1. Resume headline
2. Key skills (10–15)
3. Current designation, company and total experience
4. Current and preferred locations
5. Notice period
6. Profile summary
7. Employment details for each job
8. Education and certifications

## A simple weekly routine

- **Once a week:** make one real update (a new skill, a project, a certification, a refined summary line).
- **Every few days:** check recruiter messages and reply, even with a polite no.
- **Before each important application:** tailor the uploaded resume to that job and check the auto-filled fields again.
- **Every three months:** re-read your headline and key skills against five current job ads for the role you want, and update the words.

You can run your Naukri resume through a free FixxMyResume check before uploading to see how cleanly it reads and which keywords are missing.

## Common Naukri mistakes

- Generic headline
- Key skills copied from someone else's profile
- Profile and resume showing different titles or dates
- Wrong notice period
- An old resume file from two jobs ago
