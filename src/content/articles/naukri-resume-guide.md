Naukri works two ways: you apply to jobs, and recruiters search its resume database for candidates. The second is where most experienced hires come from, and it depends on your **profile fields**, not just the PDF.

## 1. Upload a resume that parses cleanly

Use one column, standard headings and contact details in the body. Check the auto-filled fields after upload: if your job titles or dates are wrong, the layout is confusing the parser. See the [ATS checklist](/articles/how-to-make-resume-ats-friendly/).

## 2. Write a specific resume headline

Recruiters see it in search results. Make it role + experience + skills:

> Data Analyst with 4 years in retail | SQL, Power BI, Python | Pune

Not: "Seeking a challenging job". Examples: [Naukri headline and profile summary](/articles/naukri-resume-headline-and-profile-summary/).

## 3. Fill key skills carefully

Recruiters filter by skills. Add 10–15, using the common names recruiters type: "Tally ERP", "GST", "Power BI", "Java", "Spring Boot", "B2B Sales", "Key Account Management". Remove skills you no longer use.

## 4. Employment details

For each job, add designation, company, dates and a short description with two or three achievements. Keep titles and dates identical to your resume and LinkedIn.

## 5. Profile summary

Three to five lines on your experience, domain, top skills and best results. This is your pitch to a recruiter who opened your profile.

## 6. Location, notice period and salary

- **Current and preferred locations:** recruiters filter by city.
- **Notice period:** be accurate; many searches filter by it.
- **Current and expected salary:** fill these honestly; you can choose visibility settings.

## 7. Keep it active

Update your profile now and then: a new skill, a project, a certification. Respond to recruiter messages; it keeps your profile active.

## 8. Apply smart

- Read the job title and requirements; apply where you match most of them
- Tailor the uploaded resume for big applications ([15-minute method](/articles/tailor-resume-to-job-description/))
- Answer screening questions accurately

## Common Naukri mistakes

- Generic headline
- Key skills copied from someone else's profile
- Profile and resume showing different titles or dates
- Wrong notice period
- An old resume file from two jobs ago
