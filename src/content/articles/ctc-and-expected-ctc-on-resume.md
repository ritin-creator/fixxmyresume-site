## Why not on the resume

- The same resume goes to different roles with different salary bands
- A low current CTC can anchor the offer down
- Salary is private, and resumes get forwarded

## Where to share it instead

- **Job portals:** salary fields with visibility settings
- **Application forms:** the dedicated fields
- **Email to a recruiter:** a short line, if they ask

> Current CTC: ₹6.2 lakh per year (₹5.4 lakh fixed + ₹0.8 lakh variable). Expected: ₹8.5–9 lakh. Notice period: 30 days.

## Know your CTC breakdown

Check your offer letter or salary slip for fixed pay, variable pay, employer PF, gratuity and other components. Recruiters may ask for a breakdown and proof (salary slips) later in the process.

## If you must put it on the resume

Add one line at the bottom under "Additional information": "Current CTC: ₹6.2 LPA | Expected CTC: negotiable | Notice period: 30 days."
