Public sector bank recruitment runs through competitive exams and official application forms, which collect your details in their own format. This guide is for private banks, NBFCs, small finance banks and fintech companies, where a resume matters.

## Sample bullets by role (fictional)

**Relationship manager / sales officer**
- Opened 240 savings accounts in a year with ₹8 crore in balances, 115% of target
- Cross-sold credit cards and insurance to 60 existing customers
- Ranked 3rd of 45 RMs in the region for CASA growth, 2025

**Loans (home, personal, gold, MSME)**
- Sourced ₹14 crore in home loan disbursals through 12 builder and DSA tie-ups
- Kept first-EMI default under 1% by improving document checks at sourcing

**Credit analyst**
- Appraised 150+ MSME loan files a year; prepared credit notes for sanction committee
- Built an Excel checklist that cut file turnaround from 7 to 4 days

**Operations and branch banking**
- Handled cash and clearing for a branch with ₹2 crore daily cash; zero audit discrepancies in 2 years
- Processed KYC updates for 1,800 accounts during a re-KYC drive

**Collections**
- Managed a 400-account bucket with 92% resolution in a quarter

## Products and skills to list

CASA, fixed deposits, home loans, personal loans, gold loans, credit cards, insurance, mutual funds, KYC/AML, credit appraisal, Finacle or other core banking systems, CRM, Excel.

## Certifications

NISM (mutual funds distribution and others), IRDAI licensing for insurance sales, JAIIB/CAIIB for bankers.

## Freshers

B.Com, BBA or MBA freshers can show sales or customer handling from internships, part-time jobs or college fests, plus certifications. See [B.Com fresher resume](/articles/bcom-fresher-resume/).
