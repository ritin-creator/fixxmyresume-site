## Sample bullets (fictional)

- Taught mathematics to 4 sections of class 9 and 10 (160 students) under CBSE
- Class 10 board results: 96% pass rate and 21 students above 90% in 2025
- Introduced weekly practice tests that lifted the class 9 average from 61% to 70% in a year
- Coordinated the school science exhibition with 80 student projects and 600 visitors
- Trained 12 teachers to use Google Classroom during the move to blended learning

## Qualifications section

```
M.Sc Mathematics, University of Lucknow | 2016
B.Ed, University of Lucknow | 2018
CTET (Paper II, Mathematics and Science) qualified | 2019
```

## What to include

- Board (CBSE, ICSE, IB, state board), subjects and classes
- Role (PRT, TGT, PGT, class teacher, coordinator)
- Results and improvements
- Co-curricular: clubs, events, sports, olympiad coaching
- Teaching tools: smart boards, Google Classroom, Microsoft Teams, LMS
- Training and workshops attended

## Freshers with B.Ed

Use your teaching practice (internship) as experience: school, classes, subjects, lesson plans, any measurable change. Add tutoring, coaching centre work and olympiad results of students you coached.
