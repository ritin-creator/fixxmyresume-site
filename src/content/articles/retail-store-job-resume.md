## Bullets by level (fictional)

**Sales associate / cashier**
- Served 150+ customers a shift in a busy supermarket with accurate billing and zero cash shortages in a year
- Raised average bill value in the electronics section by about 12% by suggesting accessories
- Trained 3 new joiners on the billing system and returns policy

**Store supervisor**
- Supervised a team of 8 across two shifts; cut late shift starts by half with a simple roster
- Kept inventory accuracy above 98% in monthly counts

**Store manager**
- Ran a 6,000 sq ft apparel store with ₹4 crore annual sales and a 14-person team
- Grew same-store sales 11% year on year through local events and better visual merchandising
- Reduced shrinkage from 1.8% to 0.9% of sales with CCTV reviews and stock checks

## What to include

- Store format and size
- Sales, conversion, average bill value
- Inventory and shrinkage
- Team size and hiring or training
- Systems: POS, billing software, inventory apps
- Languages, shift flexibility

## Freshers

List school or college events, part-time jobs, family shop experience and any customer service. A clear, honest one-page resume with your phone number at the top is enough for most entry-level retail roles.
