## By field

**IT / software:** Java, Python, C++, JavaScript, SQL, data structures, OOP, HTML/CSS, React, Spring Boot, Git, Linux basics, REST APIs, manual testing.

**Data analytics:** Excel (pivot tables, XLOOKUP), SQL, Power BI or Tableau, Python (pandas), statistics basics.

**Commerce / accounts:** Tally Prime, GST basics, TDS basics, Excel, accounts payable/receivable, bank reconciliation, Zoho Books.

**Banking and finance:** financial statements, Excel, credit appraisal basics, KYC, mutual fund basics (NISM if certified).

**Marketing:** Canva, Instagram and Meta Ads basics, Google Analytics, SEO basics, content writing, market research.

**HR:** recruitment basics, Naukri and LinkedIn sourcing, MS Excel, onboarding, labour law basics.

**Mechanical / civil / electrical:** AutoCAD, SolidWorks, STAAD Pro, MATLAB, PLC basics, site safety, quality checks.

**Customer support / BPO:** communication in English and regional languages, typing speed, CRM basics, email writing, complaint handling.

**Sales:** prospecting, negotiation, local market knowledge, two-wheeler and licence (state it), languages.

**For everyone:** MS Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Google Workspace, languages with fluency.

## How many?

8–12. More looks padded; fewer may miss recruiter searches.

## Soft skills: prove them

| Listed | Proved |
| --- | --- |
| Leadership | Led a 12-member team to organise a 3,000-person college fest |
| Communication | Anchored 5 college events; taught 8 school students as a tutor |
| Teamwork | Built a 4-person hackathon project that placed 2nd of 60 |

## Don't list

- "MS Office" if you can only type in Word
- Skills you can't answer interview questions on
- "Hardworking, honest, punctual" as skills
