## Bullet ideas by level

**Junior / fresher**
- Built [feature] in [stack] for [users or purpose]
- Fixed [number] bugs, including [notable one]
- Wrote tests that raised coverage from X% to Y%
- Contributed [feature] to [open-source project], merged by maintainers

**Mid-level**
- Designed and shipped [service] handling [scale]
- Reduced [latency/cost/errors] by [amount] by [method]
- Owned [system] end to end, including on-call

**Senior**
- Led [team size] engineers through [project] with [outcome]
- Set architecture for [system] adopted by [number] teams
- Cut cloud spend by [amount] through [method]

## What recruiters scan for

1. Your stack, matching the job's
2. Scale (users, requests, data size)
3. Impact (speed, reliability, cost, revenue)
4. Ownership and seniority signals

## Mistakes

- Listing 30 technologies, half of which you used once
- Bullets that describe the product instead of your contribution
- No links to code or projects for juniors
- Skill bars and two-column templates
