## Sample (fictional)

```
VIKAS YADAV
Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh | +91 99350 00000 | vikas.yadav@email.com

SUMMARY
Field sales executive with 4 years in FMCG general trade and fintech merchant onboarding
across Kanpur and Unnao. Achieved over 110% of target for 3 straight years.

EXPERIENCE
Sales Executive, Northwind Foods (FMCG), Kanpur | Apr 2023 – Present
• Achieved 118% of annual volume target across a 280-outlet beat
• Added 64 new retail outlets in 12 months, including 15 in rural markets
• Cut distributor credit days from 21 to 10 through weekly collection visits

Field Sales Executive, Brightpay (fintech), Kanpur | Jun 2021 – Mar 2023
• Onboarded 900+ merchants for QR payments and soundboxes, ranked top 5 of 70 in the zone
• Activated 85% of onboarded merchants within 30 days

EDUCATION
B.A., CSJM University, Kanpur | 2021

SKILLS
Beat planning, retailer and distributor management, merchant onboarding, collections,
order apps, basic Excel
Languages: Hindi, English (working) | Own two-wheeler and driving licence
```

## Numbers that matter in field sales

| Metric | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Target achievement | 118% of annual target |
| New outlets or merchants | 64 outlets, 900 merchants |
| Territory | 280-outlet beat, 2 districts |
| Activation or repeat | 85% activated in 30 days |
| Collections | Credit days from 21 to 10 |
| Ranking | Top 5 of 70 |

## Tips

- Lead each role with target achievement
- Mention local languages and travel readiness
- Keep it to one page; recruiters for field roles screen fast, often on a phone
