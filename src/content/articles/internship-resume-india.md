## Structure

1. Name, college, phone, email, LinkedIn or GitHub
2. Objective (one line)
3. Education (current degree, year, CGPA; XII and X)
4. Projects and coursework
5. Skills
6. Activities and responsibilities
7. Certifications

## Objective examples

- "Second-year B.Tech (IT) student seeking a summer web development internship; built a college canteen ordering site in React."
- "BBA second-year student seeking a 2-month digital marketing internship; managed Instagram for the college fest, reaching 25,000 accounts."
- "B.Com student seeking an accounts internship in a CA firm; comfortable with Tally Prime and Excel."

## Turning coursework into bullets

- "Analysed 5 years of a listed company's annual reports for a financial analysis assignment; presented ratios and a buy/hold view to the class"
- "Built a library management system in Java with MySQL for a DBMS course project; handles 2,000 book records"

## Activities that count

Fest committees, college clubs, NSS, NCC, sports captaincy, student council, organising workshops. Add scale: budget, attendees, team size.

## Where to apply

College placement cell, Internshala and similar internship portals, LinkedIn, company careers pages, and direct emails to startups. For direct emails, keep it to five lines with one specific reason you want to work there.

## Mistakes

- Two pages
- "I am a hardworking student" objectives
- Listing every online course you started
- No phone number or an email like coolguy99@
